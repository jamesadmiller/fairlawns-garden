Suggested bed groupings

Soil & placement

The Achillea, Eryngium, Echinops, Origanum and Nepeta will actively reward poor, stony conditions and tolerate dry spells. Geranium 'Rozanne' and Scabiosa cope well in partial shade cast by Hydrangea and Magnolia. Geum and Helenium prefer some moisture, so site them in the more sheltered positions.

Planting alongside the existing shrubs

The cool blue-violet tones of Geranium, Nepeta and Scabiosa soften Hydrangea and Rhododendron without competing. The warm orange and wine tones — Achillea, Helenium, Penstemon 'Garnet' — are best grouped near Roses. Verbena bonariensis works as a see-through plant throughout, particularly in front of taller shrubs.

Half-hardy notes

Verbena bonariensis (H3) and Penstemon 'Garnet' (H4) are both worth treating as you do the tender Salvias — take insurance cuttings in August using the semi-ripe method in the propagation guide. Both will often come through a UK winter unscathed, but the cuttings guarantee continuity.
